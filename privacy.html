Privacy and terms

Halvia tools are concept demos with synthetic data. They are not medical devices and must not be used for real patient care. Please don’t enter real patient or personal information.

Who runs this site

halvia.cloud is run by Shaun Finch, a designer based in Manchester, UK. Contact: shaundavidfinch@gmail.com.

What this site collects

  • No accounts on a server, no cookies for tracking, and no analytics.
  • The demo tools run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to me.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Halvia bot

  • Halvia bot is a web app. When you choose to download an AI model, the files come from Hugging Face, which may log that request under its own privacy policy.
  • After that, the model runs on your device. Your questions are not sent anywhere.
  • Profiles, documents and chats are stored in your browser on this device, and the bot is designed to encrypt data locally with a passcode. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you email me

I use your email only to reply to you, and I don’t share it.
