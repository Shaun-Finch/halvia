# About Halvia bot

Source: Halvia. This is the user guide for Halvia bot.

## What Halvia bot is
Halvia bot is an AI assistant for clinicians that can work with no internet connection. The AI model runs on your own device, inside your browser, and answers using a library of documents: the Halvia starter library and any papers, guidelines and notes you add yourself.

## Working offline
The first time you turn on the AI, your device downloads the model once (about 0.9 GB for the fast model). After that it is stored on your device, and Halvia bot keeps working in airplane mode, on a ward with no signal, or in a basement clinic. If the AI is not downloaded yet, or your device cannot run it, Halvia bot still answers by searching your library and showing the passages it found.

## Adding your own documents
Sign in to create a private profile on this device, then open the Library and add PDFs, Word documents (.docx), text and Markdown files, web pages saved as HTML, or pasted text. Halvia bot splits each document into passages and indexes them, so answers can quote and cite them. This is how you teach Halvia bot: it does not change the model itself, it gives the model the right material to read before it answers, and it shows you which passages it used.

## Privacy
Your profile, documents and chats are stored only on this device and are encrypted with a key made from your passcode. Nothing you type or upload is sent to Halvia or anywhere else. If you forget your passcode, the data cannot be recovered. Do not enter patient-identifiable information.

## Safety
Halvia bot is a prototype. It is not a medical device and must not be used to make clinical decisions. Small on-device AI models can be wrong, even when they sound confident. Always check answers against the cited source, current national guidance and your local policies, and use your own clinical judgement.
