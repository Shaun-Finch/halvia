# Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) and ACVPU

Source: Teasdale G, Jennett B. Assessment of coma and impaired consciousness: a practical scale. The Lancet, 1974, and the Glasgow Coma Scale structured approach (glasgowcomascale.org). Summary written by Halvia.

## Eye opening (E)
4: opens eyes spontaneously. 3: opens eyes to sound. 2: opens eyes to pressure. 1: no eye opening.

## Verbal response (V)
5: orientated. 4: confused. 3: words. 2: sounds. 1: no verbal response.

## Motor response (M)
6: obeys commands. 5: localising to pressure. 4: normal flexion. 3: abnormal flexion. 2: extension. 1: no motor response.

## Recording the score
Report the three components as well as the total, for example E3 V4 M6 = 13. The total ranges from 3 to 15. Record NT (not testable) when a component can't be assessed, for example verbal response in an intubated patient. A GCS of 8 or less usually means the patient cannot protect their own airway.

## ACVPU
ACVPU is a quicker assessment used in NEWS2: Alert, new Confusion, responds to Voice, responds to Pain, Unresponsive. Any score other than Alert scores 3 in NEWS2.
