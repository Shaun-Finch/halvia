# Intravenous fluids in adults

Source: based on NICE clinical guideline CG174, Intravenous fluid therapy in adults in hospital (updated 2017). Summary written by Halvia. Follow your local fluid prescribing guidance.

## The 5 Rs
Think about Resuscitation, Routine maintenance, Replacement, Redistribution and Reassessment. Assess fluid status using history, examination, observations, fluid balance, weight and blood results.

## Resuscitation
If a patient needs fluid resuscitation, give a bolus of 500 mL of crystalloid containing sodium in the range 130 to 154 mmol/L (such as Hartmann's solution or 0.9% sodium chloride) over less than 15 minutes. Reassess with ABCDE after each bolus, and give further boluses if needed, up to 2000 mL, before seeking expert help. Use smaller boluses (250 mL) in people with heart failure.

## Routine maintenance
For routine maintenance, start with 25 to 30 mL/kg per day of water, about 1 mmol/kg per day each of potassium, sodium and chloride, and 50 to 100 g per day of glucose to limit starvation ketosis. Consider prescribing less (20 to 25 mL/kg per day) for people who are older or frail, have kidney impairment or heart failure, or are malnourished and at risk of refeeding syndrome.

## Monitoring
Review IV fluid prescriptions at least daily, with fluid balance, weight, and urea and electrolytes. Stop IV fluids as soon as the patient can drink enough.
