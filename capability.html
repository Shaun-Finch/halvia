Principles

  • A person makes the call. AI drafts and flags; named staff approve.
  • Show the why. Every flag links to its source sentence or rule.
  • Privacy first. Designed with UK GDPR, DPIAs and the DSPT in mind.
  • Safety designed in. Hazards logged early to support DCB0129, DCB0160 and DTAC.
  • Accessible. Plain English, reading-age checks, WCAG 2.2 AA.
  • Measure, then trust. Outputs scored against clinician-written rubrics and checked for fairness.
