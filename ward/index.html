Ward overview

Every bed's NEWS2 score, trend and next observations at a glance. Press Live and the demo clock runs: observations arrive at the frequency NEWS2 sets, and anyone who needs escalating rises to the top.

For nurses in charge and outreach teams. Final call: the registered nurse.

Early warning

Record a set of observations and NEWS2 scores them as you go. The trend across the shift and a ready-to-edit SBAR escalation sit underneath. Save to the chart and the ward board updates.

For ward nurses. Final call: the registered nurse.

Handover

Free-text notes into SBAR, with safety flags for allergies, falls, anticoagulants, delirium and results to chase. Each section is approved before sign-off.

For nurses and doctors at shift change. Final call: the clinician handing over.

Discharge letters

Ward discharge notes into a structured letter for the GP: diagnosis, what happened, medicine changes, actions for the GP and what the patient was told. Every line links back to its source.

For discharging doctors and pharmacists. Final call: the discharging clinician.

Clinical coding

Suggested ICD-10 codes with the sentence behind each one. Conditions that were ruled out are spotted and excluded.

For clinical coders. Final call: the accredited coder.

Missed appointments

Who might struggle to attend tomorrow's clinic, why, and the help to offer: a text, a video appointment or an earlier date. Never used to overbook.

For outpatient booking teams. Final call: the booking team, with patient choice.

Patient letters

A clinic letter rewritten for the patient, with jargon explained, a medicines table, next steps and a reading-age check. It can read the letter aloud.

For clinicians writing to patients. Final call: the clinician signs off the patient version.
